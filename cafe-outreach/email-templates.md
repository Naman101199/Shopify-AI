## Gmail Label Pipeline

Apply these labels to track each cafe thread:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `TRT Cafe Outreach/1 - Identified` | Cafe added to list, not yet contacted |
| `TRT Cafe Outreach/2 - Contacted` | Initial email sent |
| `TRT Cafe Outreach/3 - Replied` | Cafe responded (positive or neutral) |
| `TRT Cafe Outreach/4 - Meeting` | Call or in-person meeting scheduled |
